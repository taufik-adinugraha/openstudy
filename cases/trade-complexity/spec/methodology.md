# Methodology — Indonesia in the Global Trade Network (draft)

Becomes the public methodology page. Must state, with numbers:

1. Sources & lineage: BACI 202601 (Etalab 2.0, through 2024), Comtrade
   free-tier pulse (display-only plane), BPS reconciliation.
2. Scope: GOODS ONLY — BACI has no services trade.
3. Method: Atlas-style sample filter (pop >= 1M, trade >= $1B), Balassa RCA,
   M matrix (RCA >= 1), eigenvector ECI/PCI (py-ecomplexity + Atlas-convention
   sign normalization), proximity phi, MST + phi >= 0.55 product space,
   seeded layout.
4. Gate results G-B1..G-B4, published pass or fail — including where our
   numbers diverge from the Harvard Atlas and why.
5. The paradox, honestly: processed-nickel exports surged (USITC) while the
   ECI rank declined ~6 places over the decade — both shown, neither hidden.
6. Downloads + citation list (BACI/CEPII, Atlas, USITC, py-ecomplexity).
