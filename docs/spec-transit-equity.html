Transit Access & Urban Equity

TransJakarta, KRL, MRT and both LRTs on one routing graph with every footpath in Jabodetabek: who can reach work and a hospital within an hour on public transport, who cannot — and how unequally that hour is distributed across ~1,500 kelurahan.

G1 · The question

“An hour on public transport buys a Menteng resident half the city's jobs. What does it buy in Bekasi?”

Jakarta has spent a decade building transit — BRT to Mikrotrans, MRT, two LRTs, a commuter rail carrying a million people a day. The question every planner, employer and property investor asks is not “how long is the network” but “who can actually reach what”. This case computes door-to-door travel times at scale (graph routing over GTFS + OSM, frequency-aware) and turns them into an equity statement: cumulative access to jobs and healthcare per kelurahan, its Lorenz curve, and where poverty and disconnection coincide — the cross-case link to Case F's poverty surface.

G3 · Pipeline

Pipeline — static rebuild

Total raw pull ≈ 1.3 GB. Wall time on the 16 GB server: ingest ~30 min, network build 5–10 min, the 1,500 × 12,000 × 3-scenario matrix 20–40 min on 8 cores inside a 10 G heap, everything after that seconds. A full rebuild is comfortably under two hours.

ingest

GTFS (TransJakarta + Bogor angkot), Java PBF → osmium bbox clip, WorldPop, GHSL POP/NRES, healthsites + hospital lists, COD-AB ADM4 → Jabodetabek filter, Case F estimates. Manifest with sizes + sha256 + feed Last-Modified.

rail

gtfs_rail.py builds frequency GTFS per rail line from config.RAIL_LINES (headways re-read from the cited official pages at build time), stops/shapes from OSM relations, transfers at the named hubs. Output labelled hand-encoded.

network

r5py TransportNetwork over the clip + all GTFS zips; build report (node/edge counts, GTFS validation warnings from gtfs-kit) kept for G-G3.

matrix

Origins = ~1,500 kelurahan population-weighted centroids; destinations = 500 m hexes (~12,000) + every hospital/puskesmas; scenarios all / no-rail / walk-only; percentiles p50/p75 over the departure window.

access

Cumulative opportunities at 30/45/60 min (jobs proxy = NRES surface; hospitals count; population reach) + gravity variant (half-weight 45 min) so the cutoff choice is shown not to drive conclusions.

equity

Lorenz + Gini + Palma of population-weighted access; access vs Case F poverty (Spearman); double-disadvantage list; DKI vs Bodetabek; mode-layer attribution (Gini with/without rail, with/without TransJakarta).

validate

Gates G-G1…G-G4 → stats.json.

export

View-models ≤ 3 MB (kelurahan GeoJSON, access ledger, per-origin isochrone hex lists as lazy-loaded files, network geometry, equity curves). Insight brief per D11.

G4 · Validation gates

Validation gates

G-G1 — Timetable sanity (hard). For the published-time OD sample (MRT Lebak Bulus → Bundaran HI < 30 min official; TJ Corridor 1 Blok M → Kota ≈ 49 min from brtdata.org's 19 km/h × 15.48 km; KRL Bogor → Jakarta Kota and LRT Jabodebek end-to-end read from the operators' timetables at build): r5 in-vehicle time within ±15 % or ±8 min, whichever is larger.
G-G2 — External routing check. 50 random OD pairs vs Google Routes transit (on-the-fly, never stored raw): median absolute deviation ≤ 10 min; the full deviation distribution is charted in the methodology, including the tail.
G-G3 — Network integrity (hard). ≥ 98 % of GTFS stops snap to the street graph within 200 m; zero unreachable kelurahan origins in the WALK+TRANSIT scenario; r5 build warnings triaged and listed.
G-G4 — Plausibility (hard where bookkeeping). Rail scenario ≥ no-rail scenario everywhere (monotonicity — exact); DKI core > Bodetabek periphery on population-weighted median access; the ITDP People-Near-Transit replication (population within 1 km of ≤ 15-min-headway service) lands above the 2016 anchors — Jakarta 44 %, Greater Jakarta 16 % — since the network has only grown; the comparison is charted, not asserted.
G5 · Dashboard

Dashboard — page anatomy

Map rule: the user's browser runs with WebGL off — every map ships a full-function 2-D canvas fallback (Path2D + isPointInPath, the nightlights pattern), tagged visibly when active.

  1. Hero — “The hour.” Signature moment: from a single origin (Bundaran HI), the 60-minute isochrone blooms outward along the network — rail lines igniting in their mode colours as they're reached, the reachable area filling like ink in water. 2-D fallback: pre-rendered 5-minute isochrone rings crossfading on canvas. Reduced motion: final isochrone, static.
  2. Chapter 1 — “One network, four systems.” The assembled graph: BRT corridors, Mikrotrans capillaries, KRL trunks, MRT/LRT — each layer toggling in with its route-km and headway signature; the hand-encoded-rail caveat stated here, first.
  3. Chapter 2 — “An hour from home.” Kelurahan choropleth of 60-min jobs access; the Menteng-vs-Bekasi pairing as annotated callouts; cutoff scrubber 30/45/60 showing the cliff beyond the rail shadow.
  4. Chapter 3 — “Who is left out.” The Lorenz curve draws itself; Gini and Palma numbers land; double-disadvantage kelurahan (poor + disconnected, via Case F) highlighted on a linked mini-map — the sentence a policy client remembers.
  5. Chapter 4 — “What each layer buys.” Access with and without rail / TransJakarta: the marginal contribution of every system, as small-multiple maps + a Gini delta chart.
  6. Explore. Click any kelurahan → its isochrone (precomputed hex lists), access panel (jobs %, hospitals, nearest-hospital time, poverty estimate), scenario + cutoff toggles, URL state, chart/table + download per Grapher affordances.
  7. Methodology. Scheduled-not-congested disclosure; hand-encoded rail table with sources and the ±15 % caveat; gate results incl. the Google-comparison distribution; GTFS licence note + attribution (TransJakarta, KCI/MRT/LRT timetables, OSM ODbL, WorldPop, GHSL, healthsites ODbL, COD-AB); the RWI rejection note.

G6 · Design brief

Design brief

Identity: the transit map itself — metro-line chromatics on graphite. The accent is TransJakarta's red-orange; each mode keeps a fixed line colour throughout (BRT red-orange, KRL commuter blue, MRT teal-green, LRT gold, walk grey) so the map legend is the brand. Access ramps run graphite → red-orange; poverty stays in Case F's ochre where it appears, so the two dimensions never share a hue.

G8 · Risks & decisions pending

Risks, unknowns & decisions pending user verification

  • TransJakarta GTFS licence is unstated (feed, Transitland and Mobility Database all silent) — already in the build-plan risk register; proceed with attribution + seek written confirmation. If refused, the case survives on rail + OSM but loses its richest layer.
  • Hand-encoded rail is the weakest data — headways drift with GAPEKA revisions; every rail time carries the ±15 % caveat and the build re-reads the cited pages. An official KAI/MRT feed would retire this entire risk.
  • Jobs proxy honesty — NRES built-up surface is floorspace, not employment; POI density is the second proxy; both are disclosed as proxies and the access metric is named “access to job-dense floorspace”, never “jobs”.
  • Pending user verification: (1) drop of Meta RWI (CC BY-NC) in favour of the Case F cross-link — creates a build-order dependency F → G; (2) hand-encoded rail GTFS as acceptable methodology for a public demo; (3) Google Routes as validation-only comparator within the free tier (10,000 Essentials calls/mo) and ToS §19; (4) whether Kepulauan Seribu is excluded from equity aggregates (boat-only access distorts Gini).
  • Out of scope: congestion-adjusted times, fares/affordability, park-and-ride, future-network scenarios (an obvious paid-pilot upsell, noted in the CTA).
