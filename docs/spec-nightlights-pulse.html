A1 · The question

“Which of Indonesia’s 514 regencies are accelerating or stalling — months before official statistics say so?”

The client-facing promise: official regional GDP (PDRB) arrives annually, 6–12 months late. Satellite radiance arrives monthly. We calibrate the relationship where it is strong, state plainly where it is weak, and deliver a regency-level activity pulse the audience can interrogate. The honesty is the pitch: a client who watches us bound our own claims trusts the rest of the site.

A3 · Pipeline

Pipeline — monthly DAG

GitHub Actions cron, ~90 min, idempotent by month key. If EOG hasn’t published the month yet, the run exits cleanly and the site keeps its “data through …” stamp.

ingest

Download the month’s Black Marble tiles (h27–h32/v08–v10) from LAADS with the Earthdata bearer token — VJ146A3 from 2018-01, VNP46A3 for earlier months, both during the overlap era. HDF5 → Indonesia-clipped COGs in R2 (raw/bm/YYYY-MM/). Ocean-only tiles 404 and are skipped; checksum-verify; retry ×3.

mask

Null out cells failing the composite’s own QA layers (observation count < 2, mandatory-quality flags); clamp negative radiance to 0; apply 5 km gas-flare buffers (EOG VNF static catalog). Log masked-area share per month as a pipeline health metric.

zonal

exactextract sum-of-lights, mean radiance, and lit-area share per regency against the frozen boundaries → one row per regency-month appended to the parquet ledger (DuckDB-managed, ~514 × 170 months).

deseason

Per-regency STL decomposition plus a Hijri-calendar regressor: Ramadan moves ~11 days earlier each Gregorian year and visibly shifts lighting, so each month carries its Ramadan-overlap fraction as a covariate. This detail is shown off in the story — it reads as “insightful,” because it is.

calibrate

Annually: levels cross-section log(PDRB) ~ log(SOL) per year (the R² gate lives here) + within-regency panel elasticity with confidence intervals, reported honestly (~0.2 expected per the literature).

nowcast

Pulse Index per regency = deseasonalized 3-month radiance growth mapped through the panel elasticity, with uncertainty band. Presented as a lights-implied activity index, never as “GDP.” Rolling backtest 2016–2024 vs later official PDRB releases.

anomalies

STL-residual z-scores → quarterly “fastest risers / fallers” board, filtered to |z| ≥ 2 sustained ≥ 2 months.

brief

Insight brief generated from the computed-stats JSON only (low-temperature LLM call, fixed template, no free retrieval). The brief lands as a PR — merging it is the human review. Unmerged brief = site shows the previous one.

publish

Export monthly PMTiles (choropleth), parquet extracts, and JSON view models; static build; deploy to Cloudflare Pages with data-vintage stamp.

A4 · Validation gates

Validation gates

G-A1 — Levels fit. Cross-sectional log(SOL) vs log(PDRB) across ~500 regencies: R² ≥ 0.65 for every year 2015→latest. Scoped to levels only, per Gibson et al. 2021 — the panel elasticity (~0.2) is displayed, with CIs, not gated.
G-A2 — Nowcast skill. Rolling out-of-sample backtest: provincial Pulse Index growth must beat the naive last-value baseline on MAE for ≥ 60% of provinces. Published on the methodology page either way.
G-A3 — Narrative sanity. The series must show, unprompted: the 2020 COVID collapse and recovery, Morowali’s smelter-driven surge, IKN construction ramp, and the Ramadan cycle. Any absence is a bug until explained.
G-A4 — Cross-sensor checks. (a) Splice integrity: VNP46A3 and VJ146A3 national monthly series correlate ≥ 0.95 over their overlap, and the inter-calibration leaves no visible step at the 2018 seam. (b) Independent source: annual national totals correlate ≥ 0.9 with the manually downloaded EOG VNL annual composite. Divergence blocks publish (sensor drift or pipeline error).
A5 · Dashboard

Dashboard — page anatomy

Canonical structure: dark WebGL hero → four scroll chapters → free-explore instrument. Every chart: hover inspection with real values, shareable URL state, chart/table toggle, data download (Grapher affordances).

  1. Hero — “From 830 km up.” Black screen, one line of copy, then Indonesia’s lights fade up over 3 s (pre-rendered annual-composite frames cross-faded as WebGL textures; static image under prefers-reduced-motion). A scrub hint invites dragging through 2012→now.
  2. Chapter 1 — “Lights track money.” Animated scatter: log SOL vs log PDRB, one dot per regency, colored by island group, per-year animation with the fitted line and live R² readout — gate G-A1 rendered publicly as the trust move.
  3. Chapter 2 — “The pulse.” National + provincial deseasonalized index against official GDP growth: COVID dip annotated, Ramadan wiggle explained inline with the Hijri regressor.
  4. Chapter 3 — “Where it’s happening.” Camera flights to Morowali (nickel smelters — cross-links to Flagship B), IKN Nusantara, Bali’s tourism recovery; small-multiple light curves with event annotations.
  5. Explore — the instrument. Full-screen MapLibre + deck.gl choropleth of the Pulse Index; month slider with play; regency click → drawer (light curve vs PDRB bars, rank, anomaly flags); pin-two compare mode; search; full state in URL.
  6. Brief + methodology. “This month in lights” (labeled generated · reviewed, with timestamp); methodology page carries the full method, limitations (elasticity honesty, DKI service-economy caveat), EOG/NASA attribution + logo, downloads, citations.

A6 · Design brief

Design brief

Identity: city lights seen from orbit. Dark-first; the data ramp is the aesthetic — the same black→indigo→amber→white ramp as the imagery itself, so the interface and the satellite agree.

ground #0A0E1A surface #131A2E starlight text #E9E4D6 sodium amber #E8A33D slate (declines) #6B7CA6 teal (official series) #4BB8A9
  • Amber is reserved for lights-derived data and rising values; official-statistics series always render teal — the two data provenances are color-coded site-visitors can learn once.
  • Type: shared lab system — Archivo (display), Public Sans (UI/body), IBM Plex Mono (data, axis, stamps), tabular-nums throughout.
  • Motion: one hero reveal; one camera flight per chapter; 200–300 ms ease-out micro-interactions; nothing loops except the optional time-scrub play.
  • Light theme (fallback): warm paper ground, same amber/teal semantics at darkened, AA-contrast values.

A8 · Risks & out of scope

Risks & out of scope

  • LAADS publication delay — some months publish late; the clean-exit design makes this a non-event, and the vintage stamp keeps it honest.
  • Splice inter-calibration (D12) — the 2018 VNP46→VJ146 seam must not read as an economic event; gate G-A4a exists precisely for this, and the methodology page shows the overlap fit.
  • Earthdata token rotation — tokens live ~60 days; the CI secret must be regenerated on schedule or the cron starts no-op’ing (current token expires 2026-10-28).
  • DKI divergence — Jakarta’s service economy weakens the lights link locally (published finding); the drawer for DKI carries an explicit caveat chip.
  • Out of scope: ask-the-data Q&A (phase 2), per-capita welfare claims, sub-regency estimates, any use of the word “GDP” for the Pulse Index.
